## Rejected / not used

- Commercial recordings: not searched/downloaded.
- YouTube extraction: not used.
- Spotify or streaming-platform audio: not used.
- Vivaldi "Free Tim" Commons/FMA candidate: not used because the Commons page had CC0 text but embedded metadata referenced a noncommercial share-alike license and the page carried an audio license-review-needed category.
- Debussy YouTube-import candidate: not used because a clearer Commons-hosted CC BY recording by Laurens Goedhart was available.

## Operational note

Vivaldi Spring is represented as one work with three local movement files from the same credited recording set. No audio was joined or converted.
